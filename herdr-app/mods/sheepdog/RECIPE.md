# Sheepdog

A docked rail and a floating panel for [sheepdog](https://github.com/kccarlos/herdr-sheepdog), the daemon that lets you manage your coding agents by voice. The rail says whether the voice is listening, muted or off, who holds it ("Voice on iPhone"), and how many agents need you. Clicking it opens the panel: a window with the conversation you also see on the phone, a composer to type to Sheepdog, and the questions and suggested replies to tap. (A `preview.png` is added with the screenshots; there is none yet.)

## What you see

**The rail**, docked on the right at 200 px on the `chrome` background. At 160 px or wider it is a column with 12 px padding and 10 px gaps:

1. The colour logo (22 px) and "Sheepdog" (13 px semibold).
2. The state, one of:
   - Looking for the daemon: "Looking for Sheepdog…" in `dim`.
   - Daemon down: "Sheepdog isn't running" (13 px semibold), "Start it with:", the command `sheepdog setup` in a `raised` box with a 1 px `border`, and "Looking again every 3 seconds." in `dim`.
   - Setup needed: "Setup needed" in `blocked`, then the daemon's own words (its voice `problem`, or "Run sheepdog setup to add a voice key.").
   - Otherwise: a 9 px dot and the voice's words in 13 px semibold, an optional `muted` note, and either "N agents need you" (`blocked`, 12 px medium) or "Nothing is waiting on you." (`dim`).
3. When the daemon answers but its event stream is down: a dashed 1 px `border` line, "Live updates paused", in `muted`.
4. An **Open panel** button (28 px high, 1 px `border`; `solid` with `solid_foreground` while an agent needs you).

Under 160 px it is a compact column: the logo (26 px), the voice dot, and, when agents need you, a `blocked` badge with the count; the whole column is the button and its tooltip says the state. The dot is `done` while listening, `idle` while muted, `working` while connecting, speaking or waiting for the voice, `blocked` in setup needed and `dim` otherwise.

The voice's words, from the daemon's `voice` in `/api/state`: "Voice off", "Connecting", "Voice not connected", "Waiting for the voice", "Ready" (nobody holds the voice), "Listening", "Muted", "Speaking" (this Mac holds it), and "Voice on iPhone" (or "…on a browser", else the client's name), with ", muted" or ", speaking" added, when another device holds it. This Mac is the holder whose `voice.client.platform` is `macos`.

**The panel**, a 420 × 640 window on the `chrome` background:

1. A header, 12 px padding, a 1 px `border` under it: the logo (20 px), "Sheepdog" (13 px semibold) and under it the voice dot (7 px) and words (11.5 px, `muted`); a `blocked` pill ("1 needs you", "2 need you") with `solid_foreground` text when agents need you; a **Technical** text button (11.5 px, `muted`); and a ✕ that closes the window. These stop the press so the rest of the window drags.
2. A banner when it applies: the daemon-down text as the whole body (title, the reason when something other than sheepdog answered, "Start it with:", `sheepdog setup`, a primary **Run it in a tab** button, "Looking again every 3 seconds."); the setup-needed block under the header (title in `blocked`, the daemon's words, a primary **Run sheepdog setup** button); the dashed "Live updates paused" line.
3. The timeline, newest first, with a scrollbar. Each day has a heading, "Today", "Yesterday" or "Monday 21 September" (with the year when it is not this one), between two hairlines, above that day's rows. Times are 24 h local, "17:42". Rows by category (docs/dev/design/conversation-timeline.md):
   - **Dialogue**: bubbles, 14 px radius with one 6 px corner, at most 4/5 of the width. You are on the right (`accent`, `accent_foreground`), the voice on the left (`raised`, `foreground`). A 10.5 px `dim` caption says "Typed" under your typed lines and "Sheepdog wrote" under a reply that was written.
   - **Needs you**, the only amber: an open question, or a push that interrupts, as a pointer: `blocked` at 12% fill, 1 px `blocked` at 33% border, 10 px radius, a 8 px `blocked` dot and "Codex asked — waiting for your answer" (the agent's name, else "An agent"), the question's words under it in `muted`.
   - **Outcomes**, lines of a ledger: consecutive lines share one `raised` card with a 1 px `border` and 10 px corners at the ends of the run. A 28 px well leads each: a round one with `>_` (`working` on `working` at 15%) for an agent's news, `✓` (`done` on `done` at 15%) for an approved question, `×` (`foreground` on `hover`) for a declined one, a hollow `border` ring with `–` (`muted`) for one that expired, was replaced or closed; a square well with `▢` for a card. The line is the agent's name (11 px, semibold, `muted`) over the words (12.5 px) over the time (10.5 px, `dim`). A settled question reads "You approved — Yes, proceed · by tap" or "Codex's question (allow Computer Use to use Finder) — closed".
   - **Context**: a hairline with a centred 11 px `muted` caption: a day, "New conversation · 17:42", "Sheepdog restarted · 17:42", "Sheepdog updated to v0.9.1 · 17:42", and "Voice restarted — it may not remember the last minute · 17:42" when the voice lost its context within 5 minutes of dialogue.
   - **System** (reconnects, a closed voice session, kinds this build does not know): not listed, except under **Technical**, where every row shows with its three-letter code (DLG, ASK, UPD, CTX, SYS) in a gutter and a dashed 1 px `border` box for the plumbing.
   - The last row says "Earlier messages", "Loading earlier…" or "This is the start of the conversation" in a dashed box.
4. With no history: "Nothing has been said yet." centred in `dim`.
5. Under the list, when the daemon answers (also in setup needed), from the top:
   - **Open questions** (the daemon's `questions`, else `pending_action`; only worker prompts with options), a card each with 8 px between: a 7 px dot (`blocked`; `done` once settled), "Codex needs your answer" ("Codex asked" once settled; "An agent" without a name) at 12 px semibold, the question's words (12.5 px semibold), the command (11.5 px monospace in a `raised` box), and one full-width button per option: `raised`, 1 px `border`, 8 px radius, the label (12.5 px semibold), a "Recommended" tag (`blocked` at 12% fill) when the agent recommends it and the description under it (11.5 px, `muted`). No option is filled. The first card has a 2 px `blocked` border, the others 1 px `border`. A tap sends `POST /api/pending/answer`; the buttons show "Sending…" and wait for it; the card then reads "✓ You chose Yes" (`done` border) and stays four seconds after the daemon stops listing it. A 409 reads "That question was already answered or has gone." and asks `/api/state` again; any other error shows under the buttons and leaves it open.
   - **Compact**: when the cards' estimated height passes 55% of the window's height (back to inline under 85% of that, and never while the questions sheet is open or an answer is on its way) they are one 36 px bar, `blocked` at 12% with a 1 px `blocked` 33% border: a dot, "Codex asked" ("2 questions need you") and "Answer" in `blocked`. It opens the cards over the list, with a **Close** button. The heights are estimated from the words and the window's width (the runtime has no measuring a render may use), a simplification of the phone's measured rule.
   - **The composer**, 12 px padding and a 1 px `border` above: when a suggested reply is live, "Suggested replies" (10.5 px, `dim`) over outline buttons that wrap; then the textarea (1 to 5 rows, 13 px) in a `input` box, 1 px `border`, 8 px radius; then the hint "Enter sends · Shift+Enter for a new line" (10.5 px, `dim`), "Waiting for the reply…" while a line is on its way, or the note of the last send: an error in `blocked` ("the orchestrator is off (…)", "Sheepdog did not answer. Your text is kept; send it again to retry.") or "Answered by voice." in `muted`, which goes after six seconds.
6. A card the orchestrator showed is a ledger line with a `▢` well, its title, "Card · Codex · 17:42" and a `›`. Pressing it (the list's `on_item_click`) opens the card in place of the list: a bar with the title, "Card · Codex" and **Close**, then the body, then, when the card has a `pane_id`, a primary button "Show Codex in herdr" (a `blocked` line under it says when herdr-app refused). A `card` is markdown: headings in bold, bullet and numbered lists, fenced code in a `raised` monospace box, tables as a `raised` box with a bold header row and hairlines (`:---:` alignments kept), paragraphs; bold, italic and links are shown as their words. An `excerpt` is the agent's screen lines in monospace, an `approval` card its question, command and options read-only ("Answer it in the question card."), an `agent` card its title.

Every colour is a `theme()` name, so dark and light both work; the only computed colours are `blocked` and `working` and `done` with an alpha suffix. The logo is `logo.svg` with its cream sticker outline, drawn with `image()` (which keeps colour; `svg()` would tint it to one).

## How it behaves

- The data layer asks the daemon: `GET /api/state` and `GET /api/conversation?limit=50` (and `&before=<next_before>` for older pages) with `fetch`, and `GET /events?kinds=card,conversation,daemon,pending,voice,web&since=<newest item id>` over a raw TCP socket (`net.connect`), because `fetch` is buffered. The overlays also read `/api/agents`, the voice and orchestrator model routes, `/api/settings`, `/api/devices`, `/api/pair` and `/api/pair/qr.json`; their changes use the matching `PUT`, the confirmed device `DELETE`, and the other routes described below. The mod reads the chunked HTTP body itself and parses `id:`, `event:` and `data:`. Every record, the `: keepalive` comments the daemon sends every 15 s included, proves the stream is alive; after 40 s without one it reconnects, waiting 1 s, then 2, 4, 8 and 15 s while that fails, and sends `Last-Event-ID`.
- An event that can change what is shown (a question, a push, an utterance, a reset, a restart, who holds the voice, mute, speaking) makes it ask `/api/state` and `/api/conversation` again, at most four times a second; transcript fragments wait for 1.5 s of quiet. Other events (audio stats, playback, usage) change nothing. Items merge by id, the server's copy winning.
- While the stream is down it asks every 3 seconds instead and shows "Live updates paused". If nothing answers the daemon is down, with the same 3 s retry.
- The list pages back when its last row is drawn: it asks for the next older page (`before`), and the rows are added below, so nothing moves under you.
- **Technical** shows every row the daemon sent; without it only what was said, what needs you, what agents did and where you are.
- The header's **⋯** menu opens three separate overlays: **Settings** (voice and orchestrator models, languages, reply style, paired devices with a two-step Unpair, and `/api/state`'s update notice), **Pair a phone** (a QR drawn with `div()`s from `/api/pair/qr.json`, the `/api/pair` link as plain fallback text, and **New code**, which calls `/api/pair?new=1`), and **New conversation** (a confirmation before `POST /api/conversation/reset {via:"app"}`). AI-service consent is phone-local today and has no daemon route, so this desktop Settings has no consent toggle.
- A press on a timeline row that names an agent opens its details from `GET /api/agents`: what it works on, its last result and detail, its last question and answer, and its space. A card row still opens the card instead.
- Clicking the rail (or **Open panel**) opens one window with `key: "sheepdog-panel"` and `keep: true`: opening it again replaces it, and it returns after a restart. The ✕ closes it for good. `mod.open` opens the same panel as a dialog without that key.
- The buttons **Run it in a tab** and **Run sheepdog setup** run `terminal.open` with `argv: ["sheepdog", "setup"]` in a tab.
- **Sending.** The textarea's `submit` event sends, never `change` (an input method's commit fires `change`). A textarea inserts the Enter's newline and also fires `submit`: with shift nothing is sent (the newline stays); without it the value minus ONE trailing newline is the text and the box is cleared; a value that is only whitespace never sends (and is cleared). `POST /api/turn {text}` carries an `Idempotency-Key` (`panel-<time>-<n>-<random>`). One line at a time: while one waits for its reply, Enter keeps the text.
- **Pending and reply.** The line shows at once as a dimmed bubble captioned "Sending…"; when `/api/turn` answers, the reply shows as a `dog` bubble ("Sheepdog wrote") and the panel asks `/api/conversation`. The daemon's own items are the record: the pending line is dropped when a typed `shepherd` item with the same words from the send on is in the conversation, the reply when a typed `dog` item after it has the reply's words (matched by id, each item claimed once), so nothing shows twice. One the conversation never shows goes after two minutes.
- **Failures.** 503 (the orchestrator is off) shows the daemon's reason under the box and puts the text back; 409 shows "Answered by voice." and keeps nothing (a spoken turn took the words); no answer at all shows "did not answer", keeps the text and the key, so sending the same text again replays the reply if the daemon did run the turn. After any status reply the next send gets a new key: the daemon keeps a refusal under its key too. A failed suggested reply leaves its button live.
- **Suggested replies** are the newest orchestrator reply's `options`, from its `voice.tool_result` event (the stream's one payload the panel reads; `voice` is already in `kinds`). Only the newest reply is live, and only until the user's words (said, typed, tapped or still pending), a newer reply (options or not), a new conversation, a daemon restart or the voice losing its context. A tap sends the option as a typed line. A reply that arrived before the panel was open, and is older than the newest conversation item, is not seen; it is not offered.
- It sends the three POSTs above, all with an `Idempotency-Key`: no `/ws`, no keys, no files. Nothing is written inside the mod folder at run time (herdr-app reloads a mod when its folder changes).

## Built from

- `main.js`: one `View`, `Sheepdog`. The docked block is the rail; an overlay's `role` is the panel or one of the menu, Settings, pairing, agent-details and New-conversation overlays. The rail's copy asks only for the state. Imports `View` from `gpui-kit`, `TextareaState` from `gpui-base`, `connect` from `net`, and from `herdr`: `closeOverlay`, `openOverlay`, `props`, `run`, `shared`, `theme`. It starts the live data layer only for the rail and panel and stops it in `close()` (which also releases the textarea); the panel makes the textarea, the outbox and the answers in `init`; `cx.timer.every(250, …)` pages back and drops what has stayed long enough; `.on_size_change` switches the rail between wide and compact and tells the panel its size for the compact rule.
- `mod.json`: `{ "place": "right", "size": 200, "title": "Sheepdog", "overlay": { "width": 420, "height": 640 } }`.
- `net/`: `config.js` (the base URL, default `http://127.0.0.1:7777`, from the block's `props.url` or the shared key `sheepdog.url`; only plain `http`), `http.js` (the GETs, model and settings `PUT`s, device `DELETE` and three POSTs, each POST with an `Idempotency-Key`), `sse.js` (chunked decoder, SSE parser, the client with its 1 s silence check, and the backoff), `live.js` (events and the 3 s poll drive the store).
- `model/`: `classify.js` (the phone's classification table, its notices and the settled-question words), `timeline.js` (day headings, dividers, the reconnect and settled-question rules, ledger joins, spacing, newest-first layout), `history.js` (pages merged by id), `store.js` (with the replies folded from events), `status.js` (the voice's words, the states), `events.js` (which events matter, and the `kinds`), `composer.js` (what `submit` sends, what a failed send gives back, the keys), `outbox.js` (the lines on their way, matched against the conversation by id), `questions.js` (the open questions and their answer phases), `presentation.js` (inline or compact), `replies.js` (suggested replies and their liveness), `cards.js` and `markdown.js` (a card's words, its agent, its markdown blocks), `settings.js` (the loaded form, action/error state, language order and update words), `qr.js` (validated matrix rows), `agents.js` (the phone's detail fields). None imports `herdr`.
- `ui/`: `rail.js`, `panel.js` (`list()` with a `Scrollbar.vertical` of the same id and `on_item_click`), `rows.js`, `composer.js` (`Textarea` and the suggested replies), `questions.js`, `cards.js` (the card sheet), `widgets.js`, and the separate `menu.js`, `settings.js`, `pair.js`, `agent.js` and `reset.js` overlays with `overlay-widgets.js`. They use `div`, `image` and `list` from `gpui-kit`, `h_flex`, `v_flex`, `Textarea` and `Scrollbar` from `gpui-base`.
- `logo.svg`, drawn with `image("mods/sheepdog/logo.svg")`.
- Network and programs, to say before installing: `fetch` and one raw TCP socket to the daemon address only (default `127.0.0.1:7777`, this computer; nothing else is contacted, no token is sent); the daemon routes above when their screens or actions are used; `run("terminal.open", { argv: ["sheepdog", "setup"], place: "tab" })` when you press a setup button; and `run("agent.focus", { terminal })` when you press **Show … in herdr** on a card. It reads no files and writes none.

## Watch out

- **Newest first, still, with the composer at the bottom.** Chat order (newest just above the composer) was looked for and not found. `list()` ignores `track_scroll` (`herdr-app check` warns), so code cannot scroll it to the bottom; `v_virtual_list` takes a scroll handle (`scroll_to_bottom`) but wants each row's height, which wrapped text and cards do not have. The other candidate, a scroll area (`overflow_y_scroll` with an id) with `flex_col_reverse` and the rows reversed, builds every row (no lazy rows, so no "last row drawn" to page back on), starts at its top edge, and a reversed column's overflow lies above that edge, where a scroll offset does not reach; `herdr-app check` renders only the rail, so this cannot be shown to work without a person looking at the open window, and nothing here ships on a guess. If a later herdr-app lets `list()` anchor to its end (or a handle on it), switch `newestFirst` in `model/timeline.js` off and put the composer's pending line last.
- The rows of a `list()` may carry no handlers; a press on a row is the list's `on_item_click(key)` (the row id), which is how a card row opens. The question cards, the suggested replies and the composer are outside the list for the same reason. The phone's "N agent updates" group (it opens on a tap) is not drawn; every update is its own line.
- The textarea state (`TextareaState.new`) needs a live host call, so it is made in `init`, never in `render`, and released in `close()`. A mod folder write resets it (herdr-app reloads the mod), which is why nothing is ever written there.
- `herdr-app check` renders only the docked rail, with no daemon. The panel, its rows, its overlays and the daemon states are only drawn when opened: look at them in herdr-app.
- `fetch` cannot stream (buffered, 8 MiB, 30 s) and the mod runtime has no `setTimeout`: the stream is `net.connect` plus `cx.sleep`, and a read that never returns cannot be given a timeout, so a watchdog task checks the silence every second. Closing the socket may not wake a pending read; the client races the read against the watchdog and `stop()`.
- No `TextDecoder` is assumed: `net/sse.js` decodes UTF-8 by hand. No `Intl` either: times are `HH:MM`.
- Each open copy of the mod has its own state, stream and poll: the rail and the panel are two connections. A copy hidden in a column keeps its stream; it asks nothing while the daemon is quiet.
- The panel lists only what `GET /api/conversation` has (items with server ids) and the lines it is itself waiting on. The orchestrator's spoken replies and held pushes come over `/ws`, which this mod never opens; its suggested options are read from the `voice.tool_result` events on `/events`.
- A card's agent is focused with `run("agent.focus", { terminal: pane_id })`: with the herdr-core backend a card's `pane_id` is the agent's terminal address, which is what herdr-app calls its terminal. herdr-app says nothing back if no such agent is open; the mod shows a note only when `run` throws.
- Only plain `http://host[:port]` is read: the raw socket does not speak TLS.
- The rail docks only into a layout you own; in a shipped layout use `mod.open`.

## Done when

1. `herdr-app check herdr-app/mods/sheepdog` passes with no daemon running, and `make test-panel` passes.
2. With no daemon on the address the rail says "Sheepdog isn't running" with `sheepdog setup`, and the panel says the same with a **Run it in a tab** button; starting the daemon turns both into the voice state within about 3 seconds, with no restart.
3. With a daemon the rail's voice words match `/api/state` (`voice.mode`, `status`, `muted`, `speaking`, `client`): with the phone holding the voice it says "Voice on iPhone".
4. A blocked agent adds a `blocked` count to the rail within about a second and a pointer to the panel; answering it elsewhere turns the pointer into a settled line.
5. The panel's timeline matches the phone's for the same daemon: the same categories, settled questions as one line, the reconnect rule, restarts as dividers.
6. Reaching the bottom of the list loads the next older page until "This is the start of the conversation".
7. Stopping the daemon shows the daemon-down state; restarting it brings the timeline back without reopening the window. "Live updates paused" shows only while `/api/state` answers and `/events` does not, and goes when the stream is up.
8. Reloading the mod twice leaves one panel window; closing it with ✕ and restarting herdr-app does not bring it back.
9. Nothing was written inside the mod folder and no request other than the routes above and their three POSTs (`/api/turn`, `/api/pending/answer`, `/api/conversation/reset`) reached the daemon.
10. Typing a line and pressing Enter shows it dimmed at once and the reply when it comes, once each after the conversation refreshes; Shift+Enter makes a new line; an empty box sends nothing; committing text with an input method does not send it, and the next Enter does. With the orchestrator off the reason shows and the text stays.
11. A blocked agent's question shows as a card with a button per option; tapping one answers it (the agent goes on, the card says what you chose and then goes); answering it from the phone first shows "That question was already answered or has gone." A long question with long options shows as the one bar instead.
12. After the orchestrator suggests next steps, they are buttons; tapping one sends it as a typed line; once you or the voice say anything else they are gone.
13. Pressing a card row opens the card (a table is a table); **Show … in herdr** brings that agent's terminal forward.
14. The **⋯** menu's Settings shows the voice and orchestrator models, languages, reply style and paired devices, makes each change and shows a refusal in the daemon's words. A device asks before it is removed; the update notice matches `/api/state`.
15. Pair a phone draws the matrix from `/api/pair/qr.json` and shows the plain link; a phone scans it, and **New code** replaces the old one.
16. Pressing a timeline row that names an agent shows what it works on, its last result, its last question and answer, and its space.
17. New conversation asks before it clears Sheepdog's and the voice's context; canceling sends nothing, and confirming sends `{"via":"app"}` once.
