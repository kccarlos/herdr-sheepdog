# Sheepdog panel for herdr-app

A [herdr-app](https://herdr.dev) mod for [sheepdog](../../../README.md), the daemon that lets you manage your coding agents by voice. It shows the conversation you also see on the phone: what you and the voice said, the questions that need you, what your agents did. You can type to Sheepdog, answer its questions, tap its suggested replies and control the voice (Talk, Mute, Stop, hold to talk) from it. Talking itself needs Sheepdog.app (the menu-bar app); the panel never opens the microphone. The design is [docs/dev/design/desktop-client.md](../../../docs/dev/design/desktop-client.md) (sections 6 to 8).

- **The docked block is the whole panel** (340 px on the right by default). Top to bottom: the status (whether the voice is listening, muted or off, who holds it, "Voice on iPhone", and how many agents need you, in amber), any banners, the voice controls, an agent's question as a card with a button per option (when they would take too much of the block it is one bar, "Codex asked · Answer", that opens them), the composer with the orchestrator's suggested next steps as buttons, and under them the timeline, newest first with every item, older history added as you reach the bottom. The timeline always keeps at least 55% of a short panel: banners collapse to one line and the stack above the timeline gets its own scroll before the composer can be pushed away. **Technical** adds the plumbing (reconnects, restarts) the timeline hides; consecutive restart dividers become one. In the composer **Enter** sends and **Shift+Enter** starts a new line. Your line shows at once, then the reply; if Sheepdog cannot take it (the orchestrator is off, nothing answers) the reason shows under the box and your text stays. A card the orchestrator showed (a table, a list) opens when you press its row, with a button that shows the agent in herdr.
- **Voice controls.** **Talk** starts Sheepdog.app on this Mac when nobody holds the voice (it runs `open sheepdog://talk`) and unmutes when it is muted; **Mute**; **Stop**, only while it speaks; **Hold to talk**, open from the press to the release. They go through the daemon, which relays them to whoever holds the voice, so they work for the phone too. When the iPhone holds the voice the status says "Voice on iPhone" and **Use here** takes it back to this Mac. "No voice is connected" (a 409) offers **Use here** as well. With no Sheepdog.app in `/Applications` or `~/Applications` (a read-only look) the buttons are off and the panel says `brew install --cask sheepdog-app`; typing still works.
- **Settings and details.** The header's **⋯** menu opens Settings (voice and orchestrator models, languages, reply style, paired devices and the update notice), Pair a phone (the QR and its plain link), and New conversation (a confirmation before the reset). Pressing a timeline row that names an agent opens what it works on, its last result, last question and answer, and its space.
- **The rail.** A sidebar narrower than 160 px shows a compact rail instead (the logo, a voice dot and a needs-you badge); clicking it opens the same panel as a window, which drags, remembers where you dropped it and comes back after a restart.
- **Not docked?** `herdr-app do mod.open --name sheepdog` opens the panel as a window, with a "Put Sheepdog in the sidebar" hint on top (see Install).

## Install

Needs the sheepdog daemon on the same computer (`sheepdog setup`; default `http://127.0.0.1:7777`) and a herdr-app with mods. Copy this folder to `~/.config/herdr-app/mods/sheepdog/` (leave `README.md`, `RECIPE.md` and the `*.test.mjs` files out if you like), then `herdr-app check ~/.config/herdr-app/mods/sheepdog`. herdr-app docks it the first time it sees the folder, into a layout you own (`mod.json` asks for the right side, 340 px). A layout that ships with herdr-app cannot be changed, and a mod cannot change a layout itself (`layout.set` takes a whole tree, and only for layouts you own), so there the window opened by `herdr-app do mod.open --name sheepdog` offers **Copy my layout** (`layout.clone`: it switches to a copy that is yours and closes the window); then add a block for the mod `sheepdog` on the right side of that copy (the layout files are in herdr-app's folder, `layouts/<name>.json`) and it is docked. The hint was written from herdr-app's own command notes, not tried on every layout. Written and checked with the runtime `herdr-app check` reports as gpui-shell 0.7.1.

To point it at another address, set `url` in the layout block's `props` or the shared key `sheepdog.url`. Only plain `http://host[:port]` works (the stream is read over a raw socket).

## What it does on your computer

It only talks to the daemon address:

- `GET /api/state`, `/api/conversation`, `/api/agents`, `/api/voice/providers`, `/api/brain/models`, `/api/settings`, `/api/devices`, `/api/pair` and `/api/pair/qr.json` with `fetch`, plus a `GET /events?kinds=…` stream over a raw TCP socket. No token and no cookie: the daemon treats this computer's loopback address as the local device. It never opens `/ws`, because a second voice client would replace your phone.
- `PUT /api/voice/provider`, `PUT /api/brain/model` and `PUT /api/settings` for changes in Settings; `DELETE /api/devices/{id}` after a paired device's removal is confirmed; and `POST`s with `fetch`, each with an `Idempotency-Key`: `/api/turn` with a line you typed (or a suggested reply you tapped), `/api/pending/answer` with the option you tapped, `/api/conversation/reset` after New conversation is confirmed, and `/api/voice/mute`, `/api/voice/stop` and `/api/voice/ptt` for the voice controls.
- **Talk** and **Use here** run `open sheepdog://talk` (`exec`), which macOS routes to Sheepdog.app. It looks (read-only) whether `/Applications/Sheepdog.app` or `~/Applications/Sheepdog.app` exists, every 30 seconds.
- **Copy my layout** in a window that is not docked runs herdr-app's `layout.clone`.
- The **Run it in a tab** and **Run sheepdog setup** buttons run `sheepdog setup` in a new terminal tab (`terminal.open`). **Show … in herdr** on a card runs herdr-app's own `agent.focus` for that agent's terminal.
- The pairing QR is drawn from the daemon's module matrix with `div`s; its plain fallback text is the pairing link. It reads no file contents (only whether Sheepdog.app is there) and writes none: herdr-app reloads a mod when its folder changes, so nothing is ever written inside it.

## Develop

`net/` and `model/` import nothing from herdr, so plain node tests them: `make test-panel` from the repository root (it is part of `make test-web`, which CI runs). The composer's rules, the outbox, the questions' phases and compact rule, the suggested replies' liveness, the vertical layout budget, the Settings form's state and choices, the QR matrix rows, the agent-detail fields, the layout rule (panel or rail by width), the amber and the voice controls' state table and command mapping are in `model/` for that reason. The light-theme choices still use `theme()` tokens. The timeline's classification is shared with the iPhone app through `ios/Tests/Fixtures/timeline-classification.json`: change `model/classify.js` or `ConversationTimeline.classify` and that file together. `herdr-app check herdr-app/mods/sheepdog` loads the mod and renders the docked panel once, with no daemon and no network; it writes `gpui-kit.d.ts` and `jsconfig.json` into the folder, which `.gitignore` leaves out. The rows, the rail, the overlays and the window's docking hint are only drawn with data or at another width, so look at them in herdr-app too.

[RECIPE.md](RECIPE.md) is the brief to build this mod from scratch.
