## The lesson from three turns of the voice layer (CEO, 2026-10-04)

1. **v1 started as two brains** — and the voice model "said nonsense": it had seven action tools and too little context, so the weaker judge held the side effects (docs/history/issues-2026-09-18.md).
2. **Then the voice model became a mouthpiece** — and it turned out to have its own ideas anyway (answering from memory, "On it.", relaying pushes back).
3. **Then guardrails were added to restrain it** — and that overshot into code that interprets intent.

The gap was never "too much or too little trust in one place"; it is that the harness must **trust the models' judgment as the core** and do two things only: prevent runaways, and **when something happens, put an update into the model's context saying what happened, where it came from, and how it relates to what came before**. Claude Code's own harness is the model to copy: when another agent relays a message, the harness wraps it with a note that it came from an agent, not the user, and is reference only; the model chooses priority (now/next/later) and decides what to do with it. Sheepdog's equivalents: every briefing to the fast brain and every event to the slow brain carries provenance (who: CEO / brain / worker / daemon / another agent), time, and its relation to earlier items (answers your question from 12:03; supersedes the status at 12:01; the CEO heard only the first half; this is the 2nd reminder about the same prompt). The difference from v1 is structural: the fast brain still has no action tools, and it now gets rich, labelled context instead of scripts.

### Result (2026-10-04, #134 #135 #136; details in docs/research/live-resume-context-2026-10-04.md)

None of the four suspects. **A resumption handle issued by a connection that was itself resumed restores only what was said on that connection, not the context it inherited.** The first resume of a session is lossless; every later one keeps only the last connection's content (canary chain arm: 9 of 9 runs, 1,444 → 364 tokens, 0/8 facts recalled; both modalities collapse; later handles and `transparent` do not help). The daemon's logs agree: 7 first resumes ratio 1.00–1.35, 23 later resumes median 0.73. Separately, past the 16k compression trigger the oldest facts are dropped with or without a resume (1/9). Reproducer: `go run ./cmd/livecanary -arm chain -chain-fill 0 -chain-talk`. Options for the CEO (none implemented): (1) never resume a resumed session — start fresh and re-seed from recap and briefings at each later GoAway; (2) resume once, re-seed on the second; (3) leave it and rely on hand-off to the slow brain; (4) report to Google with the reproducer, meanwhile 1 or 2.
