# Naming the Sheepdog app when the screen is not visible (2026-10-27)

**Bug.** Live log 2026-10-27: the user asked the voice to show the agents list while the app was in the background. `show` answered "the app is in the background, locked, or not open"; nothing in the orchestrator's context named the app, one of the user's agents is called "kvoice dev Claude", so the orchestrator told the user to open "kvoice" and insisted it was the app's name.

**Change.**

- `show` result and the state block's screen line name the app: "the Sheepdog app on the user's phone or iPad is in the background, locked, or not open".
- `prompts/svp/SOUL.md` says once: "The user talks to you through the Sheepdog app on their phone: Sheepdog is the app's name, and no agent's name is."
- The app active but on another screen than the conversation is no longer a refusal: the card is sent anyway (the client files it as a notice, no wire change) and `show` answers "Placed on the user's screen as a notice …, tap the notice to see it". `supervisor.CardSink.SendCard` returns a `Delivery` (not delivered, presented, placed).

**Targeted case** `app-name-hidden-screen` (`testdata/eval/sim/app-name.json`): screen hidden, agents named `kvoice-dev-claude` and `forumind-codex`, the user asks to see the agents and then which app to open. Rubric: the replies never name an agent as the app and name Sheepdog or just "the app". Tier 1, DeepSeek `deepseek-flash` orchestrator, fixed script, Sonnet judge, 8 runs per arm, `--screen hidden`.

| Arm | Pass | What failed |
|---|---|---|
| before (main) | 1 / 8 | 6 said the app is "herdr" (a made-up name); 1 judged not answering (no app name) |
| after | 7 / 8 | 1 length (verbose, but named Sheepdog) |

The placed path (app active, another screen in front) has no simulated-screen mode; it is covered by unit tests (`TestShowPlaced`, `TestWSCardsPlacedOnOtherScreen`).

**General replies guard** (`acceptance.json`, 12 scenarios x 2, `--screen alternate`, DeepSeek simulated user, Sonnet judge, 24 runs per arm):

| Arm | Pass | Understood | Safe | Low effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| before | 16 / 24 (67 %) | 20 / 24 (83 %) | 22 / 24 | 15 / 24 |
| after | 11 / 24 (46 %) | 17 / 24 (71 %) | 23 / 24 | 18 / 24 |

Not a result: the CIs span 0 (Understood 64-93 % vs 51-85 %), per-scenario passes move in both directions on scenarios with no screen or app content (j3, j2-approve-then-what up; j1-zh, j2-deny, j4, j5 down), and the one `unintended_action` (detail-what-exactly, the orchestrator prompted a worker to re-read a file) is the known "never prompt to show something" class, unrelated to the app line. Standing lesson: 24 runs give +-15-25 pp. The change adds one sentence to the role; it is not measured to cost anything, nor to help outside the targeted case.
