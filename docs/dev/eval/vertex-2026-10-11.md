## Decision (CEO rule: adopt an arm only if it wins segment Understood without a Safe regression)

**Neither arm is adopted.** Segment Understood does not move beyond its CI for either arm (rl -3 pp, CI -16 to +11; nc +2 pp, CI -10 to +15). Neither shows a Safe regression. Direction only:

- `nc` (never compress the orchestrator's text) is the one that moves explanation: the voice says 0.79 of the orchestrator's key points against 0.50 for `a` (+0.29, CI +0.01 to +0.56), voice coverage of the world's key points 0.31 vs 0.20 (+0.11, CI -0.03 to +0.25), voice follow-up depth pass 0.71 vs 0.62 (n.s.). Voice answers are longer (en 43 vs 32 words, +11, CI +3 to +18). Understood and Safe do not drop; one watch-item: segment `said_vs_done` 88 % (52/59) vs 95 % (63/66), n.s.
- `rl` (gentle length rule) raises the voice share of the orchestrator's points less (0.71, +0.21, CI -0.07 to +0.47), hands almost nothing to the orchestrator (39/42 follow-ups answered without it, against 27/42 for `a`) and drops the control padding pass for the voice to 4/12 (a 9/12, nc 9/12).
- Worth a larger confirmation run for `nc` only: the effect is on the explain half (12 scenario runs per arm), not on the north star.

## Caveats

- **n per cell**: tier 2 has 12 workday runs and 12 explain-scenario runs per arm (plus 6 controls scored for padding only). CIs: run level ±15–25 pp, segment level ±10–12 pp (143–146 segments from 12 runs, cluster bootstrap), explain coverage ±0.1. Only `nc`'s voice-share and voice-word differences exclude zero, and with 20+ comparisons in this document one could be chance.
- **Unjudged segments**: Sonnet returned no verdict (`no verdict for right_agent`, three attempts) for: arm a workday-explain-deep r2, workday-interruptions r2, workday-scale-ten r2; rl workday-interruptions r2, scale-ten r1 and r2; nc scale-ten r1 and r2. These runs keep the fake-judge segment verdicts (everything yes), which weighs toward optimistic segment rates (3, 3 and 2 of 12 workday runs per arm). The same failure happened in the 10-08 rebaseline.
- **Timeouts**: four tier-2 runs ended in a turn timeout (arm a morning-standup r2; rl scale-ten r1 and morning-standup r1; nc long-haul r1), and tier 1 Gemini had 3 timeouts and 2 brain fallbacks. They are graded as they ended, which lowers Low effort and may affect segment rates unevenly between arms.
- **Run-level Understood in tier 2 is not a north-star signal here**: the explain scenarios' depth and padding items almost always fail for every arm (17 of 18 in arm a).
- **Orchestrator not constant**: the orchestrator is sampled, and its coverage differs between arms (rl 0.27 vs a 0.38); part of the explanation differences is brain variance.
- **One configuration**: Vertex scratch config, chattiness chatty. Adversarial and safety scenarios were not sent to Google, so Safe is about the benign workday, not the `rm -rf ./data` case (the eval index asks for 15+ scale-ten runs for a Safe claim; this has 2 per arm).
- **Tier-1 merge**: a first merge dropped `workday-explain-deep` (a script bug); the committed merged files were corrected and that part rejudged. Both merged records hold 30 runs.
- **Cost**: the live runs cost about $29.4 of the $30 cap (STATUS); judging ran on the Claude subscription, no API spend.
