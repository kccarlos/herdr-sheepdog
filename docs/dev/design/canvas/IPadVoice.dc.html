Where the voice goes on iPad: the same floating capsule as iPhone

One capsule per window, 366 × 60, floating at the bottom centre of the content area (never over the sidebar). The sidebar is navigation only. Showing or hiding the sidebar, rotating or resizing the window slides the capsule to the new centre (200 ms, brand ease; Reduce Motion: no slide). Narrower than 390 pt: width − 24.
