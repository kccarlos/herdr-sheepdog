What /factory opens when there are two or more projects and none is chosen. One card per project: its name (the folder's name), the first line of its goal, its state, its progress, and "needs you: N" when it has open asks for you. Order: projects that need you, then running, stopped, finished, and last a project that cannot be read. A card is one link into its project; the unreadable one is not a link. The header button counts every project.
